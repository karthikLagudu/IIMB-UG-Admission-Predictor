## Conflict policy

The formal procedure says Class X overall must be at least 60%; the current FAQ says Class X Mathematics must be at least 60%. The predictor treats the formal procedure as primary, reports the FAQ result beside it, and labels the conflict. Neither value is silently discarded.

## Deliberately unavailable

No source above establishes the exact UG raw-to-weighted transformation, current qualifying-pool mean/SD values, the 2027 category-wise PI-call cutoff, current final composite cutoff, programme closing scores, programme allocation sequence, or a defensible probability calibration dataset. These remain `DATA_REQUIRED`.
